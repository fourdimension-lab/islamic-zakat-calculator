The 8 Categories of Zakat Recipients (Quran 9:60)

Zakat must strictly be given to one or more of the eight classes specified in the Holy Quran:

1. Al-Fuqara (The Poor)Those with no income or wealth.
2. Al-Masakin (The Destitute)Those unable to meet basic daily sustenance.
3. Al-'Amilina 'AlayhaLegitimate appointed collectors of Zakat.
4. Al-Mu'allafatu QulubuhumThose whose hearts are to be reconciled.
5. Fir-Riqab (Freeing Captives)Assisting slaves or prisoners of war in liberation.
6. Al-Gharimin (In Debt)Those overwhelmed by genuine unpayable debt.
7. Fi SabilillahThose striving in the cause of Allah.
8. Ibn As-Sabil (The Wayfarer)Stranded travelers who lack means to return home.
